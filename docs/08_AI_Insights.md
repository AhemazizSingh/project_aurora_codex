# Purpose

The AI Insights Engine transforms financial data into clear, practical recommendations.

FinanceOS should never overwhelm users with raw numbers.

Instead of only displaying charts, it should answer:

• What happened?

• Why did it happen?

• Is it good or bad?

• What should I do next?

---

# AI Philosophy

Version 1 uses a deterministic Rule Engine.

No internet required.

No LLM required.

Fast.

Private.

Predictable.

Every recommendation must be explainable.

The user should always understand why an insight was generated.

---

# Insight Priority Levels

Critical 🔴

Important 🟠

Suggestion 🔵

Achievement 🟢

Only one Critical insight should appear at a time.

Maximum three Important insights.

Suggestions are shown only when no critical issues exist.

Achievements celebrate progress and encourage good habits.

# Action Center

The Action Center converts insights into actions.

Examples

Reduce Dining by ₹1,200.

Move ₹10,000 to Emergency Fund.

Review subscriptions.

Archive unused accounts.

---

# Monthly Financial Story

At month end, FinanceOS generates a narrative.

Example

"In July you earned ₹82,000, spent ₹39,000, saved ₹24,000 and invested ₹12,000. Your Financial Health Score improved from 84 to 89. Dining expenses decreased by 11%, allowing you to contribute more toward your Emergency Fund. At your current pace, your MacBook goal will be completed in November."

This report can be exported as PDF and shared.

# Design Rules

Never overwhelm users.

Never display more than one Critical insight.

Always explain the reason.

Always suggest an action.

Never generate advice without supporting data.

Maintain user privacy.

All insights must work offline.
